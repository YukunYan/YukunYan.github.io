About

Welcome to my academic homepage. I am interested in strongly coupled quantum systems and their nonequilibrium dynamics, particularly in holographic approaches to superfluidity and quantum turbulence.

My work combines analytical methods and large-scale numerical simulations to investigate collective excitations, vortex dynamics, and energy transport.

Research Interests

  • Gauge/gravity duality and holographic quantum matter
  • Holographic superfluids and vortex dynamics
  • Quantum turbulence and wave turbulence
  • Nonequilibrium dynamics and dissipative processes
  • Scientific computing and machine learning in physics
